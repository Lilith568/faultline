Hypotheses

Muse reads the page and proposes ways it could lose the user's work. Each one becomes an experiment.

    How a hunt works:
    1. Faultline explores the page, its hidden dialogs and API calls.
    2. Muse proposes competing hypotheses and designs one experiment for each.
    3. Playwright runs them with slowed or failing requests and checks the invariants. The model never grades itself.
    4. Muse reads the verified results and designs follow-ups.
    5. Every failure is minimized, re-run three times, diagnosed and exported as a Playwright test.
